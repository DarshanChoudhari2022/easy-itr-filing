# TaxMitra Filing Roadmap: FY 2025-26

To complete your tax filing for the period **1 Apr 2025 - 31 Mar 2026**, follow this precise 4-step workflow:

## Step 1: Initialize Your Profile
1. Navigate to the **Easy Filing Wizard** (the wand icon in the sidebar).
2. Complete the **Personal Details** section.
3. Ensure your **Assessment Year** is set to **AY 2026-27** in the Dashboard (this matches FY 2025-26).

## Step 2: Input Your Financial Data
### Crypto Assets (Section 115BBH)
1. Go to **Crypto & Coins**.
2. Click **+ Add Trade**.
3. Use the **Manual Add form** to enter your trades (Buy/Sell/Airdrops).
4. *Real-time Math:* The dashboard will immediately calculate your **Realized Capital Gains** and **TDS Summary** based on India's FIFO rules.

### Income & Savings
1. Go to **Income Tax Info** to add your Salary, House Property, or Freelance income.
2. Use the **Tax Savings** section to add 80C/80D deductions.

## Step 3: Global Asset Compliance
1. If you hold US stocks or foreign bank accounts, use the **Foreign Assets** module.
2. The system will automatically generate **Schedule FA** data, which is mandatory even with zero tax.

## Step 4: Finalize & Generate ITR
1. Go to **Home Dashboard** and click **File Now**.
2. Review the **Tax Summary** (this bridges all your Crypto and Income data).
3. Click **Generate ITR JSON**.
4. **Download Audit Pack**: Go back to Crypto -> Tax Reports and download your **Schedule VDA** PDF. This is your audit protection if you receive a notice.
