# Domain Context

## Filing Session
The saved, per-user, per-financial-year workspace that collects all answers, imports, validations, tax calculations, and generated artifacts for one ITR filing.

## Beginner Filer
An individual taxpayer who cannot be assumed to know the Income Tax portal, ITR form names, AIS/Form 26AS, tax regimes, deductions, or the order of filing steps.

## Filing Coach
The product experience that explains what to do next, why it matters, what document/source to open, and whether the current answer is safe enough to continue.

## Filing Module
A coherent part of the filing journey with its own purpose, readiness status, evidence needs, validations, and final contribution to the ITR output.

## Filing Guidance
The beginner-facing instructions, checklists, and journey steps that translate tax and portal complexity into small actions.

## Evidence
A source document, import, user answer, or calculation trace used to justify a field in the final ITR JSON.

## Data Coverage
The completeness state for imported tax data, especially crypto data: which sources are uploaded, which financial years are covered, and which gaps block a reliable computation.

## Schedule VDA
The ITR schedule for crypto/Virtual Digital Asset transfers. It needs asset-level acquisition, transfer, cost, sale consideration, and taxable income details.

## Review Gate
A blocking checkpoint that compares answers against validations, anomalies, missing evidence, and filing rules before generating the ITR JSON.
